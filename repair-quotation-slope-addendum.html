Important Information
  • Priority Recommendation: Complete slope corrections FIRST (this addendum) before other general repairs. Proper drainage is foundation for long-term structural integrity.
  • Waterproofing Method: Two-layer waterproof membrane application ensures water does not seep into concrete substrate, preventing mold, rust, and structural damage.
  • Slope Gradient: Minimum 2-3% slope is critical (approximately 1cm drop per 40cm length). Lower gradients may still allow water pooling.
  • Self-Leveling Compound: Professional-grade leveling compound used to create precise slope before tile installation. Ensures consistent drainage.
  • Water Test Verification: Each area will be tested post-completion with water to confirm proper drainage before sign-off.
  • Timeline: Estimated 2 weeks for all three areas (can be phased if preferred). Each area requires 48-72 hours curing between steps.
  • Labor Rate: RM70/hour (Johor 2026 market rate). Specialist labor for waterproofing application included.
  • Warranty: 12 months on workmanship. Waterproofing system carries manufacturer warranty (varies by product).
  • Optional Add-on: Stainless steel drain grates for AC ledge & kitchen floor (RM50-100 each) recommended to prevent debris accumulation.
